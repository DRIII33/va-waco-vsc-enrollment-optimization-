# Executive Summary: Waco VSC Enrollment Optimization

---
### **Management Analyst:** Daniel Rodriguez III

### **Date:** July 31, 2026

---

## Mission Alignment
To restore the Waco VA Regional Office's ability to provide timely healthcare enrollment determinations, ensuring Veterans receive clinical access within the 5-day SLA mandate.

## Diagnostic Findings
1. **Average Turnaround:** 9.49 days (Breach of 4.49 days).
2. **Compliance Rate:** 19.7%.
3. **Primary Root Cause:** Technical Handoff Latency (44.86% of delays) due to server synchronization gaps unique to the Waco regional node.
4. **Operational Bottleneck:** Priority Group Upgrades (13.91-day avg) due to manual staff verification surges.

## Action Plan
- **API Integration:** Replace batch transfers with real-time system handoffs.
- **Rapid Response Team:** Realign 25% of staff to specialize in Priority Group verifications.
