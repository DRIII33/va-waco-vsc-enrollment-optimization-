# Project Disclaimer


**Nature of the Project:** This project is a professional portfolio piece developed by Daniel Rodriguez III. It is designed to demonstrate technical proficiency in data engineering, cloud architecture, and management analysis within a public sector context.

**Data Authenticity & Research:** All data utilized in this repository—including the 5,000-record enrollment dataset—is **entirely synthetic**. The variables, latencies, and operational bottlenecks were engineered based on scholarly research of publicly available VHA/VBA performance metrics, federal GAO audits, and public job descriptions (Code: CBZY-13013423-26-IC).

**Institutional Representation:** This project is for illustrative and educational purposes only. It does not contain non-public agency data, nor does it represent the current operational standing, internal policies, or official data of the Department of Veterans Affairs or the Waco VA Regional Office. Any resemblance to actual persons or specific real-world cases is purely coincidental and a result of the high-fidelity simulation parameters used to mirror public sector challenges.
